# Three-minute demo

## 0:00–0:35 — The problem

Explain that directors and screenwriters often discover whether an imagined scene fits a real space only during a physical scout. StageSight helps them narrow the shortlist first.

## 0:35–1:20 — Explore a real location

Open `/en`, choose a category and select a venue. Show its Korean source name, price and original listing link. Make clear that StageSight does not invent catalogue entries.

## 1:20–2:05 — Preview the shot

Select a time of day and generate a Gemini lighting preview. Compare it with the original photograph and explain that the result is an illustration for creative planning, not a measurement or guarantee.

## 2:05–2:35 — Research practical constraints

Run the Parallel research action. Show the English summary, cited source links and an empty/unconfirmed field when the evidence does not establish an answer.

## 2:35–3:00 — Match the screenplay

Briefly show the Korean screenplay workspace recommending real venues scene by scene and refining them by budget or mood. Use English narration or subtitles. End on the concrete value: arrive at the physical scout with a smaller, better-informed shortlist.
